Hi, I'm Miraz. I build apps for iPhone and Mac, mostly sideloading and signing tools like Odysseus and AltLoad, plus Agamemnon, a free antivirus for Mac.

When an idea sticks, I also make games on Roblox. Source code for most of my work is on GitHub.

Projects

Apps and tools I've built or am working on.

  • Odysseus iOS

    On-device signer and app store for iPhone. Browse sources, then download, sign and install in one tap.

  • Agamemnon macOS

    Free, open-source antivirus for Mac with download protection, quarantine, scheduled scans and encrypted DNS.

  • Dionysos iOS

    Sign and install apps with your own Apple IDs. Manage accounts, certificates and refreshes on-device.

  • Catalyst iOS

    On-device app store for iPhone and iPad. Install, refresh and customize sideloaded apps, no computer needed.

  • AltLoad iOS

    Install AltStore on your iPhone using your own Apple ID, no computer needed.

  • 🪨

    Rock Value Estimator Web

    Upload a photo of a rock to guess its type and what it might be worth.

  • 🔨

    Hephaestus In development

    A web-based signer: sign and install iOS apps right from your browser.
