Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Nodes is a desktop app that runs on your computer. There is no Nodes account and no Nodes server. Your chats, files and sign-ins stay on your device.

What Nodes stores

Everything Nodes keeps is saved on your computer only:

  • Chats, nodes, teams and settings are saved in the app's data folder.
  • API keys and sign-in tokens are encrypted with your operating system's secure storage (Keychain on macOS, DPAPI on Windows).
  • Files your nodes create are saved in the folder you choose, by default ~/Nodes.

We never receive any of this. Deleting the app's data folder removes it all.

What Nodes sends, and to whom

Nodes only sends data where you tell it to:

  • AI engines you pick. When you send a message, it goes directly from your computer to the AI provider or tool you chose (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq, or a local model like Ollama). That provider's own privacy policy applies.
  • Search and web pages. When a node searches the web or opens a page, the request goes to the search engine or site involved.
  • Apps you connect. When you connect an account like Google, Nodes talks directly to that service.

Nodes has no analytics, no tracking and no ads inside the app.

Google user data

If you choose to sign in with Google, Nodes asks for permission to:

  • See your email address, to show which account is connected.
  • Read your Gmail messages and create drafts (gmail.readonly, gmail.compose).
  • Read your Google Drive files (drive.readonly).
  • See and edit your Google Calendar events (calendar.events).

How it's used: only when you ask a node to do something with your Gmail, Drive or Calendar. For example, you might ask it to summarise an email, find a file, or add an event. Nodes reads only what that task needs, and passes it to the AI engine you picked so it can do the task. Anything that sends, creates or changes something asks you first, unless the chat is set to Full trust.

Storage: Google sign-in tokens are encrypted on your computer. Google data is never stored on any server we run (we run none), and is never sold.

Nodes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to train AI models and is not used for advertising.

Disconnecting: click Disconnect in Nodes under Apps, which deletes the tokens from your computer. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

GitHub and Notion

If you connect GitHub or Notion, Nodes uses that access only to do what you ask in a chat, such as reading a repo or a page. Tokens are encrypted on your computer and sent only to that service.

Phone access

If you turn on phone access, your phone talks to Nodes over your own Wi-Fi network. Nothing goes through the internet or any server.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits. It doesn't collect anything you type into the app.

Children

Nodes isn't meant for children under 13.
