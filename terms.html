2What AltLoad is

AltLoad is a free iOS app. It installs and refreshes AltStore directly on your iPhone or iPad, without a computer. To do this it:

  • creates a pairing file on your device using iOS's wireless pairing;
  • connects to your own device through LocalDevVPN;
  • signs in to Apple's developer services with your Apple ID to create a development certificate, App IDs and provisioning profiles, and enables the capabilities you choose;
  • downloads AltStore or AltStore Beta from AltStore's official source, signs it and installs it on your device;
  • runs an AltServer on your device so AltStore can install and refresh apps.

We don't host, sell or distribute AltStore or any other app. We don't run accounts, and there's nothing to buy. Builds on GitHub are provided unsigned. You sign and install them yourself.

6Developer features

AltLoad includes developer tools: JIT, location simulation and entitlements. They use the same mechanisms Xcode uses, and you use them at your own risk.

  • Location simulation changes the location every app on your device sees. Don't use it to deceive people, commit fraud, cheat in games or services whose terms forbid it, or get around legal or safety restrictions. Some apps may notice it and limit your account. Stop the simulation before relying on your real location, for example for navigation or emergencies.
  • JIT attaches a debugger to an app for a moment. Only use it with apps you have the right to run this way.
  • Entitlements are enabled on your own Apple ID's App IDs. Apple decides which ones your account may use.
  • To provide JIT and location simulation, AltLoad downloads Apple's Developer Disk Image from a third-party mirror and mounts it on your device, as Xcode would.
