2What AltLoad is

AltLoad is a free, open-source iOS app. It installs and refreshes AltStore directly on your iPhone or iPad, without a computer. To do this it:

  • creates a pairing file on your device using iOS's wireless pairing;
  • connects to your own device through LocalDevVPN;
  • signs in to Apple's developer services with your Apple ID to create a development certificate and provisioning profiles;
  • downloads AltStore from its official source, signs it, and installs it on your device.

We don't host, sell or distribute AltStore or any other app. We don't run accounts, and there's nothing to buy. Builds on GitHub are provided unsigned. You sign and install them yourself.
